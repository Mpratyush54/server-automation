Your choices

Turn diagnostics off
Set PLATFORM_DIAGNOSTICS=off in the environment before running platformctl on the server. It is saved for later runs, and nothing more is sent.
How long we keep it
Diagnostics reports: 180 days. Installations: deleted after 12 months without a check-in. Account and keys: until you delete your account.
Not stored
IP addresses are not stored with your account or installations. Diagnostics never contain app data, logs, host names or secrets.
Quote requests and emails
Quote requests and messages are not linked to your account. For a copy, a correction or deletion, write to {{ privacyEmail }}.

Delete account

Deleting your account removes, immediately:

  • your account: email address, password hash, sign-up and confirmation times;
  • your sign-in sessions;
  • your license keys;
  • your installation records and all their diagnostics reports.

Copies in our daily database backups are gone within 30 days. Quote requests and emails are not linked to your account: ask {{ privacyEmail }} to delete those. Download your data first if you want a copy. This cannot be undone.
