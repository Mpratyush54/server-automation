Questions

Is anything held back on the free plan?

No. Personal includes every feature, including monitoring, logs, traces, backups and high availability. The limits are 3 servers per install and 2 installs.

What counts as personal use?

Homelabs, learning, side projects and personal websites. Running Cairn for a company or organisation, or offering it to others, needs Enterprise.

Can my company try Cairn first?

Yes. Companies can evaluate Cairn for 60 days.

How is Enterprise billed?

Per server per month, billed annually, from $9 per server. Larger fleets get volume pricing by quote.
